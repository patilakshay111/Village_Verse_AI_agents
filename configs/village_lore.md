# Anandgaon — Active Village Plots

Use these ongoing storylines. Reference them in events, conversations, gossip, and memories.

## Gram Panchayat

- **Gram Panchayat Bhavan** (Panchayat Office): meetings, registers, tender files.
- **Sarpanch Vikram Deshmukh** (Deshmukh family): influential, ambitious, balances village pride with deal-making. Chairs panchayat sabha.
- **Gram Sevak Devendra Joshi**: government appointee; handles tenders, measurements, muster rolls. Caught between honest duty and pressure from contractors and sarpanch.

## PMGSY Road & Gutter Tender (₹42 lakh)

- Work site: **Nagar Road Work Site** — kutcha road paving + open gutter along Patil farm boundary.
- **Contractor Sunil More**: won tender; accused of using cheap gravel and thin RCC cover slabs.
- **Raghunath Patil** (Patil elder): leads opposition — "We will not allow this work near our fields" — fears drainage will flood sugarcane.
- **Corruption thread**: Villagers whisper that **5% kickback** was paid to approve low-quality material; gram sevak was offered a "chai-pani envelope" to sign false measurement sheets; sarpanch says "development must not stop for one family."
- Arguments at panchayat, tea stall, and construction site. Some want jobs; some want quality.

## Secret Love Story

- **Priya Sharma** (Sharma family, ~24) and **Kiran Pawar** (Pawar family, ~26): secretly in love for months.
- They meet quietly at **Water Well** at dusk or behind **Community Hall** after events.
- Families would oppose (status, Pawar driver family vs Sharma teacher reputation).
- Only close friends suspect; gossip may expose them with distorted details.
- Emotions: hope, fear, jealousy if someone flirts with Priya.

## Daru Parties & Evening Masti

- **Country Liquor Shed** (outskirts): men gather after harvest pay or festivals.
- Topics mix **masti** (jokes, film songs, cricket bets) with **serious** talk (crop prices, debt, panchayat corruption, children's future).
- Occasional **fights** (pushing, shouting) — especially if someone insults sarpanch or brings up tender bribes while drunk.
- Not every villager drinks; religious elders disapprove but still hear gossip next morning at tea stall.

## Tone

- Natural Marathi-Hindi village mix in dialogue (Roman script).
- Humor, tension, and heart — not soap opera melodrama every line.
- Corruption and romance are sensitive; show moral gray areas, not cartoon villains.
